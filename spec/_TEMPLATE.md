# Loop teardown — <GAME>

Fixed format. The point is to extract the **reward cycle** from a game you like
so it can be rebuilt at a playable time scale, without rebuilding the game.

Fill every section. "Unknown" is a legitimate answer and more useful than a
guess — it marks the thing to go find out.

## 6. The walls

1. **Soft wall** — where progress slows and you start wishing for an upgrade.
2. **Hard wall** — where the current strategy stops working entirely.
3. **Restructure** — the move that gets you past the hard wall (not a bigger
   number: a different shape).

## 7. The oh-shit moment

The first time the player realises the system is going to beat them. **The first
upgrade belongs exactly here.** Build the ladder backwards from this point.

## 9. What we are NOT rebuilding

## 10. The headless question

One question the sweep can answer without a renderer. Must be falsifiable and
must have a number as its answer.

> e.g. "At what floor count does the wait-time curve go vertical?"

## 11. The feel question

One question only a human playing can answer. Nobody may claim to have answered
this from a chart.

> e.g. "Is watching the queue drain satisfying, or just relieving?"
